16-phase NES mastery plan

Designed for a full reset. A phase is not a deadline: repeat it until the mastery gate is green, then advance. Depth and durable recall matter more than finishing quickly.

Weekly rhythm (Khan + NeetCode hybrid)

  1. Learn (40%) — 2–4 lesson guides until you can teach the idea out loud.
  2. Drill (40%) — Easy→Hard in the problem bank; never skip explanations on misses.
  3. Exam pressure (20%) — Timed block or constructed response every week from Week 2 on.
Booking gate: Book the subject exam only after every prerequisite is mastered, spaced reviews are current, and two 150-question five-hour simulations score at least 85%.

Certification checklist (AZ)

Enroll / pathway
Complete secondary education program requirements (your master’s route).
Subject knowledge
Pass NES Mathematics (or qualify for an approved waiver if your degree/experience allows).
Professional knowledge
Pass NES APK Secondary (052), including written assignments.
Apply to ADE
Submit certification application, IVP fingerprint clearance, and any institutional recommendation.
Approved area: Mathematics
Authorizes departmentalized math in the grade band of your secondary certificate.

External supplements

  • Khan Academy Math — free video reps for any weak lesson.
  • AEPA/NES official prep — frameworks, formula sheet, tutorials.
  • ADE Educator Certification — certificates, approved areas, testing info.
  • OpenStax Algebra / Precalculus / Calculus — free textbooks for depth.

Score targets

  • Domain drills: ≥85% before leaving a domain.
  • Half-length forms: ≥85% before official-length simulation.
  • 150-question, five-hour simulations: ≥85% twice.
  • APK MCQ: ≥80% + two timed CR drafts scored against PEEL.
