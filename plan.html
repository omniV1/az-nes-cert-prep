NES mastery plans: Math + English

Two independent 16-phase tracks. A phase is not a deadline: repeat it until the mastery gate is green, then advance. You can study both in parallel from the dashboard.

ELA weekly loop (what passers actually do)

NES 301 scores application on new passages plus cheap-point knowledge. Retaking Field 02 trains letter memory. Split the work.

  1. Track A · cards (20–30 min, most days) — Quizlet Learn: flip cards, then multiple choice until a term sticks, then type it. Misses fall back to multiple choice until 100% written. Prompt type (term, definition, or usage sentence) changes so the quiz is not a fixed form. Open cards · mixed quiz to 100%.
  2. Track B · new passage (60–90 min, 4–5 days) — a randomized cluster exam assembled from original passages, not Field 02. Log the trap type, not the question number. Then drill that move on a different cluster. Cluster exam · Form C · Form B · Form A.
  3. Track C · one timed original form per week — Form A, Form B, or a hard-first official-length mock. Not Field 02 after two sittings.
  4. Phase tests · mixed application + terms — every ELA phase has a timed test that samples the bank and the matching flashcard decks. Item subset, question type, and choice order are new each sitting, so letter memory does not help. After phases 6, 12, and 16, take the checkpoint that covers everything in that block.
ELA booking signal: mid-70s+ on a first Field 02 sitting and ≥80% on an original passage set. 85% on short-stem mocks is not enough. Save Pearson’s NES 301 practice test (free at registration) for the last 10–14 days. Math still uses the 85% × two official-length sims gate.

Weekly rhythm (Khan + NeetCode hybrid)

  1. Learn (40%) — 2–4 lesson guides until you can teach the idea out loud.
  2. Drill (40%) — Easy→Hard in the problem bank; never skip explanations on misses.
  3. Exam pressure (20%) — Timed block or constructed response every week from Week 2 on.
Booking gate: Math: ≥85% on two official-length simulations. ELA: original passage set ≥80%, plus a Field 02 first sitting in the mid-70s — or both original forms ≥80% if Field 02 is already burned.

Complete secondary education program requirements (your master’s route).
Subject knowledge
Pass NES Mathematics (304) and/or NES English Language Arts (301) for each approved area you want.
Professional knowledge
Pass NES APK Secondary (052), including written assignments.
Apply to ADE
Submit certification application, IVP fingerprint clearance, and any institutional recommendation.
Approved areas: Mathematics and English
Each passed subject exam authorizes that departmentalized area in the grade band of your secondary certificate.

Score targets

  • Domain drills: ≥85% before leaving a domain.
  • Half-length forms: ≥85% before official-length simulation.
  • 150-question simulations: math 5 hours ≥85% twice. ELA: treat passage-set ≥80% plus a Field 02 first sitting in the mid-70s as the honest gate — not 85% on short stems.
  • APK MCQ: ≥80% + two timed CR drafts scored against PEEL.
